# Code of conduct

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions and any project communication.

## Expected behaviour

- Discuss technical claims with evidence. "It works for me" is a data point, not
  an argument.
- Be direct about problems without being hostile to the person reporting them.
- Respect that other machines may have different wiring and that unsupported
  hardware is not a personal failure.
- Keep reports free of private data: serial numbers, UUIDs, MLB/ROM values,
  account identifiers.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private data.
- Deliberately misleading compatibility or test claims.
- Pushing users to disable security features or to apply unverified patches to
  their production systems.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, lock or close threads and reject contributions that
violate this policy. Repeated violations can lead to being blocked from the
repository.

This policy applies only to the project's own spaces. It does not create
obligations for services the project does not operate.
